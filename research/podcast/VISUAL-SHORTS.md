## 3. Rules for our pipeline (source: one wide 4K camera, two people; synthesis of the above, not all measured)

Each rule: evidence tag in brackets; "(UNVERIFIED)" = not measured with audio or seen in <4 channels.

1. **Crop vs stack.** Default = one-person full-height crop on whoever has the floor [VS1, 7/7]. Allow a stack ONLY as a 4-8 s bridge right after a host question or in real crosstalk, A top / B bottom 50/50, with a small name label per panel [VS2, DOAC only; UNVERIFIED for engagement]. Cap ~10% of the clip duration. Optional second stack kind: B-roll top / person bottom with captions at the seam [MFM 2/7]. Do not use letterboxed wide as a body layout [VS12].
2. **During a turn.** Host on screen while the host speaks (typically the opening question), guest on screen while the guest speaks [VS3, 5/7]. If the clip opens with a host question keep it short (observed 2-7 s in Lenny, MW; one 25 s in Cyprian), put the hook title on that host frame. A clip with no host question is fine: 9 of 21 podcast-type shorts open and stay on the guest only.
3. **During a reaction.** No silent-listener cutaway was confirmed [VS4]. Default: stay on the speaker; use a listener crop only for an audible laugh / agreement beat, 1-2 s [UNVERIFIED; needs an audio re-check]. Backchannel does not cut.
4. **Cut-to-talker latency.** NOT MEASURED (no audio). Keep RESEARCH-SHORTS R6 (switch on the first word) until measured.
5. **Punch-ins.** Hard jump cuts, no animated zoom [VS5]. Scale ratio medium -> tight about 1.8-2.1x head height (measured by eye on sheets rows 10, 22). Rate for a talking head: roughly 3-10 jumps/min, never closer than ~1.8 s apart (fastest observed: row 10, 10.4/min, min gap 1.8 s); Lenny-style 0 jumps also exists. Keep the head in the upper third [VS14]. Tight and medium must come from the same 4K wide, so crop headroom matters.
6. **Captions.** Word-timed chunks of 1-3 words (PL up to 4-5 because words are longer), bold sans, one line, centre of frame at y~60-70%, below the chin, never at the bottom edge [VS7]. Pick one of: black rounded plate, or white + shadow without plate [VS8]. Active-word highlight optional (DLR grey/white, DOAC colour). Speaker colour per person is a single-channel idea (DOAC) [UNVERIFIED].
7. **Hook.** A title on screen from t=0 for about 3-8 s, mid-frame (y~50-70%), 2 lines, plate or pill, over the person (host frame if the clip opens on the host) [VS9]. Not a top header. Name tag for the guest at their first appearance, small, lower third, 2-4 s [VS10].
8. **Ending.** Invite to the full episode in the last 6-8 s: either a 5.5-6.1 s brand end card (our logo, thumbnail of the full episode, "watch the full episode" line) or an overlay bar over the talker with the channel handle [VS11]. Both observed; no loop seen.
9. **B-roll / cards.** Optional layer: 10-40% of frames when we have material (DLR, DOAC); not required for a talking-head cut (Lenny, MW) [VS6, VS16]. Our cards (research items) belong here.
10. **Length.** Do not force under 35 s; observed median 75 s [VS15].
